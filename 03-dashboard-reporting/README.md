# Dashboard Reporting

This section includes selected dashboard examples built from financial operations data. The dashboards focus on expense monitoring, procurement analytics, flagged activity review, trend reporting, and operational KPI visibility.

The goal of these dashboards was to make large datasets easier for finance and operations teams to review. Instead of digging through spreadsheet rows, teams could use KPI cards, trend charts, category breakdowns, and drill-down views to identify exceptions and follow up faster.

## Dashboard Areas Covered

| Area | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| Expense Monitoring | Flagged transactions, total transactions, flagged amount, total expense amount, and expense trends |
| Employee Expense Analytics | Employee-level expense activity, claim amounts, reimbursement activity, and monthly expense trends |
| Expense Category Analysis | Total vs. flagged amounts by expense category |
| Procurement Analytics | Flagged vendors, flagged purchase orders, PO lines, total procurement amount, and flagged procurement amount |
| Vendor and Material Breakdown | Procurement activity by material, vendor category, company code, and payment terms |
| Geographic Analysis | Spend and vendor activity by country or region |
| Trend Reporting | Time-based comparison of total vs. flagged activity |

## Selected Dashboard Examples

### 1. Operational Risk & Expense Monitoring

This dashboard brings together expense KPIs, flagged transaction metrics, geography, and year-over-year trends in one monitoring view.

**What it shows:**

- Flagged employees and approvers
- Total and flagged transactions
- Total and flagged expense amounts
- Geographic spend analysis
- Flagged vs. total expense trends

**Business value:**

This dashboard made expense exceptions easier to review by connecting KPI cards, flagged activity, geographic spend, and trend reporting in one place. It gave finance and operations teams a repeatable way to review flagged transactions without rebuilding spreadsheet filters.

---

### 2. Financial Operations & Employee Analytics

This dashboard focuses on employee-level expense activity, claim amounts, reimbursement activity, and monthly expense trends.

**What it shows:**

- Top employees by expense amount and transaction count
- Claim amount and claim count
- Reimbursement activity
- Monthly expense trends compared with employee count
- Filters by date, country, and expense type

**Business value:**

This reporting view helped finance reviewers identify high-volume employees, claim patterns, reimbursement activity, and monthly expense changes faster. It made employee-level review easier without requiring separate manual pulls for each person or period.

---

### 3. Procurement & Vendor Operations Analytics

This dashboard summarizes procurement activity across vendors, purchase orders, PO lines, total spend, and flagged spend.

**What it shows:**

- Flagged vendors
- Flagged purchase orders
- Flagged PO lines
- Total procurement amount
- Flagged procurement amount
- Vendor geography
- Time trend reporting

**Business value:**

This dashboard connected procurement KPIs with vendor, material, company code, and payment-term breakdowns. It helped turn high-level flagged amounts into specific review paths for procurement and finance teams.

---

### 4. Procurement Breakdown

This dashboard provides deeper analysis by material, vendor category, company code, and payment terms.

**What it shows:**

- Material-level flagged amounts
- Vendor category breakdowns
- Company code concentration
- Payment-term analysis
- Missing or unusual payment terms

**Business value:**

This view helped identify where flagged procurement activity was concentrated and gave reviewers a clearer path for follow-up.
